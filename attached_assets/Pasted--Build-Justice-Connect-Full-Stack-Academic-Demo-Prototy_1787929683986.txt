# Build Justice Connect — Full-Stack Academic/Demo Prototype

Build a complete, responsive full-stack web application called **Justice Connect**.

### Tagline

**Understand. Navigate. Get Help.**

This is a **college academic/demo prototype**, not a real legal service. Use **fictional SAMPLE DATA only**. Do not connect to real court databases, real government APIs, or real lawyer verification systems.

The main product idea is:

> **One simple place to understand, organize, and navigate justice-related information.**

Do NOT make the product feel like an AI lawyer or legal-advice chatbot.

---

# 1. IMPORTANT SIMPLICITY RULES

Keep the project **moderate and easy to demonstrate**.

Do NOT over-engineer it.

### Authentication must be very simple:

**Citizen**

* Email
* Password
* Minimum 8 characters
* Sign In
* Sign Up

**Lawyer**

* Lawyer ID or Email
* Password
* Minimum 8 characters
* Sign In

**Admin**

* Admin Email
* Password
* Minimum 8 characters
* Sign In

### Completely remove:

* OTP
* Phone verification
* Email verification
* Google login
* Social login
* Multi-factor authentication
* Complicated password rules
* Multi-step authentication
* Real identity verification

Use simple project-level authentication with hashed passwords and sessions/JWT.

### Frontend
